About me

Dialed by H Henry Ohler
01

The first ask

Where I grew up in Westchester, nobody really had access to these watches. You either knew someone or you didn’t. That gap is where Dialed by H came from.

My first deal turned out to be a big one. A mutual connection had just had a great year in business and wanted to celebrate. He loved the Royal Oak Offshore, told me exactly what he wanted, and I found it.

02

How I work

A recent one: a referral came in looking for a rare Rolex Daytona with a Tahitian mother-of-pearl dial. I spent about two days on it and found a complete set in amazing condition.

“Purchasing a rare Rolex Daytona from Henry was an exceptional experience from start to finish.”

Chris Marotti · Verified Client

Whether it takes two days or two months, once you ask me to find something, I’m on it until it’s on your wrist.

03

Why “Dialed”

I’ve always said I’m “dialed” when I’m locked in on something. Put that next to a watch dial and the H for Henry Ohler, and the name kind of made itself. The dial is the first thing you notice on a watch. I want the experience to be the same.

04

Off the clock

I grew up playing racket sports, so these days it’s a lot of padel and tennis. I love traveling and exploring new places, which goes hand in hand with watches. This year I got to go to Geneva for the first time for Watches and Wonders.
