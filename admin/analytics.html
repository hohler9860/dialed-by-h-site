How to read this

Attribution uses the first arrival in a browser-tab session, which expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. UTM source takes precedence over referral channel. Direct/unknown includes visits without a usable referral. Browser privacy choices, blockers and network loss can reduce event coverage. Counts will differ from Vercel visitors. Historical inquiries are not backfilled.

Use campaign links such as https://www.dialedbyhenry.com/source?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio. Use short campaign labels, never customer names, emails or phone numbers.
