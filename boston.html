An independent dealer, not a showroom

Boston has authorized dealers and grey market shops. I buy, sell and trade as an independent dealer, sourcing each piece to order rather than holding a showroom. Here is what sets it apart from the standard Boston watch buying experience.

Beyond the waitlist

Boston ADs carry multi-year waits on popular Rolex and Patek references. I source globally and skip the queue entirely.

Global sourcing

Local dealers are limited to their own inventory. My network spans trusted partners, collectors, and private sellers worldwide.

Independent authentication

Every piece is independently authenticated before it reaches you. Dial, movement, case, and bracelet are verified by trusted specialists.

No retail markup

Competitive market-based pricing without the overhead of a Newbury Street storefront. You pay for the watch, not the showroom.

Brands I source

From the most sought-after sport references to complicated dress pieces, I source the full spectrum of luxury watches for Boston collectors.
