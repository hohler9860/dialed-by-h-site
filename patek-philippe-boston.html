What proper looks like

A Patek transaction has more ways to go wrong than a Rolex one. This is what I check so you do not have to.

Papers and extracts

Certificate of Origin, service records, and for older pieces an Extract from the Archives arranged directly with Patek Philippe.

Condition, honestly

Polishing, bracelet stretch, replaced links, dial condition, movement service. Called out in writing before you commit.

Real market pricing

Nautilus and Aquanaut prices move weekly. You get this week's number, not last month's screenshot.

Consignment done right
