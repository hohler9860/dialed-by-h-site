What an independent dealer gets you

The Boston authorized dealers are great at one thing: selling you a Datejust while you wait for the Daytona. I do the other thing.

No waitlist

Steel Submariners, Daytonas and GMTs in days, not years. You pay the market price and you get the watch now.

Discontinued references

Hulk, Batman, Pepsi on Jubilee, the 116500 ceramic Daytona, older Datejusts. The references an AD cannot sell you at any price.

Trade-ins that make sense

Trade your current Rolex toward the next one. I quote the trade value and the difference up front, no games at the counter.

Authenticated, every time

Serial, reference, movement, bracelet and clasp checked before you see the watch. Polished pieces are described as polished.
