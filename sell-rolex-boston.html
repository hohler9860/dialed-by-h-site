In person, in Boston

How selling works

Three steps, usually inside a week.

1. Send the details

Reference number, a photo of the dial, caseback and clasp, and whether you have the box, warranty card and service records.

2. Get the number

I check the reference against this week's dealer market and send you an outright price and, if it makes sense, a consignment estimate.

3. Meet and get paid

In Boston we meet in person. I inspect the watch, send the wire, you see it land, done. Out of state, I send an insured label.
